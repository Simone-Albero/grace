Numeric columns

Each number becomes sign(x)·log1p(|x|), then is standardized with the mean and standard deviation below (of the logged finite values); a missing or infinite one lands on the mean, 0, and the n_missing column counts, unscaled, how many numbers of the row did (present even when none). It is a diagnostic: no distance sees it.

Categorical slots

The top_n most frequent values of a column (a missing one counts as a value) get their own one-hot slot; the others share the <other> slot. The active slot weighs sqrt(cat_cost / g), with g the chance two rows differ on the column: on average a column adds cat_cost times what a number adds, and a mismatch on a concentrated column costs more (2·weight² in squared distance).

Tree of a class

Each class is cut on its own, by geometry alone, into cells of about the same size: the units every defect is measured on. A Ward tree is built on a sample of the class's distinct rows (each weighted by its copies in the CSV), and every row joins its nearest sampled row; from the root, a node of more than max_rows rows is split into its two children, until every node left is a cell. A branch under min_rows rows is no cell of its own: its rows join the nearest cell. The cells have the same size limit in every class, so that two cells compared are sampled alike, and a defect touching a share of a cell is not diluted in a larger group. The tree drawn is this cut: its leaves are the cells, each node counts the distinct rows of the cells below it (hover for the rows of the CSV). A drawing holds at most nodes: from its top, the node with the most rows is opened first, as the cut splits; a node left closed hangs a triangle over the number of its cells, and a click on it draws it on its own, a level down. The path above the tree leads back up. Click a node to map its rows.

How to read
rows, CSV rows
Rows are the distinct rows stage clean kept; CSV rows count their copies too. Every measure is computed on distinct rows; shares of the dataset are in CSV rows.
cells and regions
Every defect is measured on the cells, compact groups of at most the same number of rows in every class (see the cells tab). For reading, neighbouring cells of a class are merged into a region up their tree, while they have the same defects between cells (class_overlap and shortcut) against the same classes; the defects of single rows (invalid_values, duplicates, label_conflict) are counted over the region. A region so hides nothing its cells show.
defects
A cell or a region has a defect when its definition below holds; there is one threshold, τ = , the share of a cell's rows that may be confused with another class's. Defects are listed in the order invalid_values, duplicates, label_conflict, class_overlap, shortcut.
invalid_values
At least one row holds a cell that is empty, not a number where a number is due (unparsable), NaN or infinite.
duplicates
At least one row comes more than once in the CSV, label included: copies can fall on both sides of a train/test split (Flood et al., EuroS&P 2024).
label_conflict
At least one row is identical on every feature, as read from the CSV, to a row of another class: no classifier can tell them apart.
rivals
The cells of other classes a cell is measured against: for each of random rows of it, the nearest row of another class; the cells those rows lie in are its rivals. Each pair is measured on at most random rows per cell.
confusion
The share of a cell's rows that the better of two simple classifiers gets wrong against a rival: the 1-NN on every column (nn_error: the row's nearest other row is the rival's) and the best rule on a single column (a threshold on a number, a value-to-class map on a categorical; OneR, Holte 1993). Any classifier errs at least as much as the best one can; the 1-NN alone overstates the error when columns that do not separate swamp the distance. bayes_error_low is the least error any classifier could reach, from the pair's nn_error (Cover & Hart; an approximation on few rows).
class_overlap
Against at least one rival, the confusion of the cell's rows is τ or more.
shortcut
Against at least one rival the confusion is under τ, but removing at most columns (the key columns, each the one whose removal raises the 1-NN error the most, chosen on half of the rows and measured on the other half) brings it to τ: the separation rests on those few columns.

Classes
