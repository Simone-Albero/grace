Numeric columns

Each number becomes sign(x)·log1p(|x|), then is standardized with the mean and standard deviation below (of the logged finite values); a missing or infinite one lands on the mean, 0, and the n_missing column counts, unscaled, how many numbers of the row did (present even when none). It is a diagnostic: no distance sees it.

Categorical slots

The top_n most frequent values of a column (a missing one counts as a value) get their own one-hot slot; the others share the <other> slot. The active slot weighs sqrt(cat_cost / g), with g the chance two rows differ on the column: on average a column adds cat_cost times what a number adds, and a mismatch on a concentrated column costs more (2·weight² in squared distance).
