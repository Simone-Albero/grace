Tree of a class

A square is a leaf, a circle a node that splits; size follows the train rows and color the measure chosen below, darker when higher in the light theme and lighter in the dark one. Hover a node for its counts and rates, click a leaf for its page.

The map under the tree places each leaf in the plane of the first two principal components of the train rows of the class, in the LDA space. A circle is centred on the mean of the leaf's train rows and its radius is their RMS distance from it. The tree also cuts on the disagreement of the neighbours, an axis the plane does not show, so the circles of two leaves can overlap. Click a node that splits to map only the leaves below it, in a plane fitted to them: far fewer circles overlap.
