Tree of a class

A square is a leaf, a circle a node that splits; size follows the train rows. Color shows the measure chosen below against the rate at the top of the tree: blue is better than it, red worse, and the ends of the scale are the 2nd and 98th percentiles of the leaves. Hover a node for its counts and rates, click a leaf for its page.

The map under the tree draws a sample of the train rows of each leaf, at most a thousand of them thinned to the same count per leaf, in the plane of the first two principal components of the class's train rows in the LDA space, each point in the color of its leaf; the view leaves out the farthest 1% on each side, and the autoscale button of the chart shows all. The tree also cuts on the disagreement of the neighbours, an axis the plane does not show. Click a node that splits to cut the tree down to its subtree, with the map refitted to the rows below it; Reset, or the class in the path above the tree, brings back the whole.
